System architecture

Prediction becomes a governed record.

Provider choice can change. Task definitions and routing rules can change. The explanation and audit path stays explicit.

Design principle

Accuracy earns authority. Explanation governs its use.

Historical predictive performance matters because it determines qualification. The runtime product is the decision trail: evidence, disagreement, policy, authority, final record, and audit.

Historical layer Qualification evidence
